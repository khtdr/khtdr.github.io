# Accounts and sign-in

## Resetting your password

Click **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and enter your email. The reset
link works for 30 minutes. If it does not arrive, check spam, then ask your
workspace owner whether your email domain blocks tally.app.

## Two-step sign-in

Turn it on in **Settings → Security**. Tally supports authenticator apps and
hardware keys. It does not support SMS codes.

## Changing your email

**Settings → Profile → Email**. You confirm the new address from the address
itself before the change takes effect.
