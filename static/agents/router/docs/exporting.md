# Exporting your data

Open **Settings → Data** and click **Export**. Tally builds a zip of every list,
task and comment as CSV, and emails you a link when it is ready. Small accounts
get the email within a minute. Accounts with more than 50,000 tasks can take up
to an hour.

The link works for 7 days. After that, export again.

Exports include archived lists. They do not include attachments; download those
from each task.
