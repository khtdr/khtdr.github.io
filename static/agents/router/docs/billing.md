# Plans and billing

Tally is free for up to 3 people. **Team** is $6 per person per month, billed
monthly or yearly. Yearly billing is two months free.

Change plans in **Settings → Billing**. Upgrades apply at once and are
prorated. Downgrades apply at the end of the current period.

Invoices are emailed to the workspace owner and are listed under
**Settings → Billing → Invoices**.
